01: Animals Screen & Housing Units

01 Navigating the Animals Screen

The Animals page is your main dashboard for seeing all housing units (pens and coops) on your farm at a single glance.

1.1 How to Access the Animals Screen

  1. Open the App Log into your Ani-Grow account.
  2. Tap the "Animals" Icon On the bottom navigation bar, tap the Animals icon.
  3. Browse Your Housing Units You will see cards representing each Chicken Coop or Pig Pen registered on your farm.

1.2 What the Housing Cards Show

Card Element What It Means
Housing Name The name or number of the pen/coop (e.g., "Coop 1", "Pen A").
Animal Icon & Tag Shows Chicken or Pig with corresponding theme colors.
Live Heads vs Capacity Shows the current live animals in the pen compared to the maximum capacity (e.g., 100 / 120 heads).
Active Batch & Age Displays the active batch name and the animals' current age in days (e.g., Day 28).

02: Housing Details & Adding a Batch

02 Housing Details & Adding a Batch

Tapping on any housing unit opens its Details Screen, where you can see detailed batch stats and register new batches when a new delivery arrives.

2.1 How to Register a New Batch

  1. Select a Housing Pen/Coop Tap on the pen card where the new animals will be placed.
  2. Tap the "Add Batch" Button Tap the Add Batch button to open the intake form.
  3. Enter the Delivery Details Fill out the simple form fields:
    • Batch Name: Name for this group (e.g., Batch 1 - Broilers)
    • Arrival Date (Arrived): 9/7/2026
    • Age at Arrival: 28 days
    • Supplier: San Miguel
    • Initial Count: 100 heads
  4. Tap "Save" The batch is now active! The app will automatically track their daily age and growth.

03: Logging Deceased Animals

03 Logging Deceased Animals

Whenever an animal passes away, logging it in the app keeps your live inventory count 100% accurate and updates your batch survival rate automatically.

3.1 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open the Batch Details Screen Tap on the pen/coop where the incident occurred.
  2. Tap the "Deceased" Button Click on the Deceased button on the batch action panel.
  3. Fill Out the "Log Cause of Mortality" Modal Enter the number of dead animals and choose the cause:
    • Illness: Sickness, respiratory, or digestive disease.
    • Injury: Physical accident, fracture, or smothering.
    • Predator: Attacked or taken by rats, snakes, or stray animals.
    • Unknown: Sudden death with no obvious symptoms.
    • Other: Culling of runts, extreme heat, or other reasons.
  4. Tap "Save Record" The app will immediately subtract the count from your Live Heads count.

04: Target Weight & Growth Curves

04 Checking Target Weight Progress

The Target Weight page lets you compare your animals' real weights (captured by the AniWeigh scale) against the official standard target curve for their breed and age.

4.1 How to Check Growth Status

When you weigh animals using your connected scale or enter a sample weight, the app places a dot on the growth curve and tags the status:

Growth Tag What It Means What to Do
Underweight Animals are below standard target weight. Check feed availability, feeder schedule, and drinker water flow.
Normal / On-Track Animals are growing at the ideal healthy rate. Keep following your normal feeding schedule.
Overweight / Ahead Animals are heavier than the standard age curve. Animals may be ready for early market harvest!

05: Harvest & Batch History

05 Harvest & Batch History

When your animals reach target market weight and are sold, you can complete the harvest in the app and review your farm's performance records anytime.

5.1 How to Complete / Harvest a Batch

  1. Open the Batch Details Screen Go to the active batch you are ready to harvest.
  2. Tap "Mark as Sold / Complete Harvest" Open the harvest completion modal.
  3. Enter the Final Sold Numbers Enter the total number of heads sold and the final total live weight (kg).
  4. Confirm & Archive The batch is safely archived in your history, and the coop is now empty and ready for cleaning and your next batch!

5.2 How to View Past Batch History

Open Batch History from the menu to see all completed cycles and review:

  • Survival Rate (%): Percentage of animals that reached harvest successfully.
  • FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio): How efficiently the animals converted feed into body weight (lower is better).
  • Total Live Weight: Total kilograms produced for that batch.
