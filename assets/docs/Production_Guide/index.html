01: Guide Screen Overview

01 Navigating the Guide Screen

The Guide tab gives you a personalized, day-by-day roadmap tailored to the exact age and species of your animals.

1.1 How to Access the Guide

  1. Tap the "Guide" Icon On the bottom navigation bar, tap the Guide icon.
  2. Select Your Active Batch Use the top dropdown to select the specific flock or pen you want to inspect (e.g., Batch 1 - Broilers (Coop 1)).
  3. Switch Species If Needed Toggle between Chicken and Pig to load species-specific standard operating procedures.

1.2 What the Screen Header Displays

Header Element Description
Current Age Badge Displays the exact day number (e.g., Day 28) calculated automatically from arrival date.
Lifecycle Phase Highlights the active growth phase: Brooder / Nursery, Grower, or Finisher.
Connected Scale / Feeder Shows linked AniWeigh scale and AniFeed dispenser status for this batch.

02: Daily SOP Checklists

02 Daily SOP Task Checklists

Every production phase contains daily Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for feeding rations, temperature management, and drinker sanitation.

2.1 How to Complete Daily Tasks

  1. Locate Today's Task Cards Scroll through the timeline to find the cards matching today's age (e.g., Day 28: Grower Feed Transition).
  2. Read the Step Instructions Review the specific requirements (e.g., target brooder temp: 32°C, drinker cleaning, feed ration change).
  3. Tap the Checkbox (Mark as Done) Tap the checkbox on the right side of the card once completed. The item turns green with a checkmark.
  4. Skipping a Task If Necessary If an action cannot be performed, tap Skip and select a reason (e.g., Weather constraints or Vet advised against).

03: AI Roadmap Adjustments

03 AI Growth Roadmap Adjustments

Ani-Grow's built-in AniBot™ AI Engine analyzes live weighing scale readings and automatically suggests adjustments when growth deviates from standard targets.

3.1 How AI Adjustments Work

Detected Condition AniBot™ AI Suggestion Benefit to Farm
Underweight Growth Lag Extends starter/grower feeding window by 3–5 days; recommends probiotic water flush. Helps lagging chicks or piglets catch up to target harvest weight without stunted growth.
Rapid Growth Ahead of Curve Adjusts finishing schedule and calculates earlier market haul readiness date. Saves feed costs by harvesting at peak FCR efficiency.

3.2 Applying an AI Suggestion

  1. Look for the "AI Insight" Banner When scale telemetry detects a growth anomaly, an AI adjustment banner appears at the top of the Guide screen.
  2. Tap "Review & Apply Adjustment" Inspect the recommended schedule change and tap Apply Adjustment to update your roadmap instantly.

04: Daily Farm Routines

04 Daily Farm Routine Checklist

To achieve maximum survival rates and peak Feed Conversion Efficiency (FCR), follow this standard three-step daily routine in the app:

4.1 Recommended Daily Routine

Time of Day Physical Farm Action App Action to Perform
Morning (6:00 AM) Check drinker water lines, feeder hopper level, and remove any mortality. Log any deceased animals via the Deceased button; check off Morning SOP tasks in Guide.
Midday (12:00 PM) Inspect coop ventilation, side curtains, and temperature sensors. Verify temperature alert notifications; check sample weights on AniWeigh scale.
Evening (6:00 PM) Confirm automated feeder night dispensing portion and secure pen perimeter. Check off Evening SOP tasks; review daily feed consumption on Analytics.
