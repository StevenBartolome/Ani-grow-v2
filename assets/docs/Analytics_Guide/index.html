01: Analytics Hub Menu

01 The Analytics Hub

The Analytics tab is your centralized reporting hub, where all weighing scale data, automatic feeder dispenses, health events, and AI forecasts are compiled into clear charts.

1.1 How to Access Analytics

  1. Open the App Log into your Ani-Grow account.
  2. Tap the "Analytics" Icon On the bottom navigation bar, tap the Analytics icon.
  3. Select a Report to View Choose from the available farm reports:
Report Title Tag What It Covers
Growth Report Growth Average Daily Gain (ADG), weight trajectories, and growth benchmark curves.
Feed Dispensed Report Feed Total kilograms dispensed, hopper fill levels, and Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR).
Health Report Health Flock/herd wellness scores, mortality cause charts, and treatment records.
Prediction Forecast AI Forecast AI-projected harvest readiness dates and estimated final market weights.

02: Growth & Feed Reports

02 Growth & Feed Reports

Understanding your daily animal growth and feed consumption helps you optimize feeding portions and reduce feed wastage.

2.1 Reading the Growth Report

Open Growth Report to inspect:

  • Average Daily Gain (ADG): How many grams your animals are gaining per day.
  • Weight Progression Curve: Line graph showing actual scale weights over time compared to standard breed targets.
  • Weight Uniformity: Distribution percentage of animals within normal weight vs underweight.

2.2 Reading the Feed Dispensed Report

Open Feed Dispensed Report to inspect:

  • Total Feed Dispensed (kg): Cumulative feed dispensed across all coops/pens.
  • FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio): Total feed divided by total weight gained (a lower number means higher feeding efficiency).
  • Daily Dispense History: Bar chart showing morning, afternoon, and evening feeding portions.

03: Health & AI Predictions

03 Health & AI Predictions

Monitor overall livestock health conditions and use AI forecasting to plan upcoming harvest schedules with precision.

3.1 Reading the Health Report

Open Health Report to review:

  • Health Status Index: Overall farm wellness score (e.g., 98% Healthy).
  • Mortality Cause Breakdown: Visual pie chart showing causes of loss (Illness, Heat Stress, Physical Injury, Unknown).
  • Active Treatments: Current ongoing medical or vitamin courses.

3.2 Reading AI Prediction Forecasts

Open Prediction Forecast Report to review:

  • Estimated Harvest Date: AI-calculated calendar date when animals will reach target market weight.
  • Projected Final Weight (kg): Estimated average body weight at harvest.
  • Growth Forecast Curve: Trajectory curve predicting remaining growth days.

04: Customizing & Printing

04 Customizing & Printing Reports

You can easily customize which coops, batches, and dates are included before printing or sharing official PDF reports.

4.1 How to Customize What to Print

  1. Select Housing Pen or Coop Use the top housing dropdown to pick a specific pen/coop (e.g., Coop 1 - Broilers) or view all coops combined.
  2. Choose Animal Species Toggle between Chicken and Pig tabs.
  3. Pick Date Range Filter by Week 1–4, Last 30 Days, or set a Custom Date Range.
  4. Tap "Print / Export PDF" Tap the printer button on the top right. The app compiles your filtered data into an official, branded PDF document!
