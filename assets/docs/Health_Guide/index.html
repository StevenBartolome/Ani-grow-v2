01: Vaccination Planner

01 The Vaccination Planner Screen

The Vaccines page allows you to view upcoming, overdue, and completed vaccinations across all your farm's coops and pens.

1.1 How to Access the Vaccine Planner

  1. Open the App Menu Tap on the Health or Vaccines icon from the main navigation.
  2. Filter by Status Use the top pill filter buttons to quickly sort your schedules:
    • All: Shows every scheduled vaccine.
    • Overdue (Red): Vaccines that passed their target date.
    • Pending (Yellow): Upcoming vaccines waiting to be administered.
    • Completed (Green): Finished and confirmed vaccine shots.

02: Adding & Completing Schedules

02 Adding & Completing Schedules

Scheduling vaccines in advance ensures your animals never miss vital immunizations (such as Newcastle, Gumboro, or Hog Cholera).

2.1 How to Add a New Vaccine Schedule

  1. Tap the Floating "+" Button On the bottom right of the Vaccine screen, tap the circular + (Add) button.
  2. Select the Housing Pen/Coop Choose which coop or pen requires the vaccine.
  3. Select Vaccine & Target Date Pick the vaccine name and use the calendar picker to select the administration date.
  4. Tap "Save Schedule" The vaccine is added to your calendar and will send automated reminder notifications.

2.2 How to Mark as Completed

  1. Tap on the Scheduled Vaccine Card Select the pending schedule once the animals have been vaccinated.
  2. Tap "Mark as Completed" Confirm the completion. The badge turns green (Completed) and updates your health compliance records.

03: Treatments & Vitamins

03 Treatments, Vitamins & Health Logs

Whenever animals receive vitamins, deworming, or medical treatments, recording them helps track farm health history and ensures proper withdrawal times before harvest.

3.1 Health Record Categories

Event Type When to Use Common Examples
Vitamins Growth supplements & stress packs B-Complex, Electrolytes, Multivitamins in water
Deworming Routine parasite prevention Levamisole, Albendazole, Ivermectin
Treatment Therapeutic medications for illness Antibiotics, Anti-inflammatory treatments
Observation Daily health checks & vet notes Mild coughing, wet litter, reduced feed intake

04: Health Analytics

04 Farm Health Analytics Report

The Health Report screen in Analytics gives you a complete bird's-eye view of flock/herd wellness, mortality breakdown charts, and AI-driven veterinary suggestions.

4.1 How to Access Health Analytics

  1. Go to Analytics Tap Analytics from the app menu and select Health Report.
  2. Review Health Metrics Check your farm's overall wellness score and review:
    • Total Health Incidents: Number of illness or treatment logs.
    • Mortality Breakdown: Pie chart showing causes (Illness, Heat, Physical, etc.).
    • Active vs Completed Events: Ongoing treatments under observation.
